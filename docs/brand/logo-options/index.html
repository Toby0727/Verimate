Verimate logo options

Round 3 adds a symmetric V (08), shown first. Below it are round 2 in red (three candidate reds, five options, and what other housing and roommate apps do) and round 1 in green. The logo’s only accent is red; everything else is ink (#111111) and white. The wordmarks are Figtree Bold turned into outlines.

Symmetric V pick: 08c · Lean V in Crimson (#B8283A). Two mirror-image strokes, one black and one red, lean in until they meet at the point: two equal roommates making one V. It’s fully symmetric, says “mates” without drawing people, and works the same way in the wordmark (the V of “Verimate” is half black, half red). It also sits on a white card, like the app. Runner-up: 08d (Held V) if you want a solid red app icon. For the plainest version, use 08a (Bold V) with the red V in the wordmark.

Round 2 pick, for comparison: 03 · Mates M in Crimson. Crimson is the red I’d use either way: white on it is 6.2:1, and it’s far from Redfin’s bright red and Airbnb’s pink-red. Signal (#CC0034) is the louder alternative, and every symmetric V is also shown in Signal.

Symmetric V

Four treatments of a mirror-symmetric V. Each one is shown at 16/32/64/128px on grey, white and dark backgrounds in Crimson, then on grey in Signal. 08a and 08c also come as a wordmark where their V replaces the V in “Verimate”. V shapes are optically lowered by about half a unit, because a V carries its weight at the top.

08a

Bold V

A clean, bold V

The plainest answer: an even, heavy V in Figtree’s shape (flat ends and a flat point), white on a red tile. It reads instantly at 16px and works with any wordmark, but it has no story of its own, and a single red letter in a tile is a common kind of mark. The wordmark lockup turns the V of “Verimate” red instead.

Mates V

Two mates make the V

Each arm is a person: a rounded body with a head at the top, and the two meet at the point, so two roommates form one V. It’s the warmest of the four and says roommates outright. At very small sizes the heads can read as two dots or a face.

Round 2: red

Round 2 recommendation: 03 · Mates M in Crimson (#B8283A). It’s the only round 2 mark that holds both halves of the name (two roommates plus a check form the M), and it uses the app’s own language: ink, white cards and a single accent.

The reds

All three pass WCAG contrast: white text and icons on them are at least 4.5:1 (normal text) and they’re at least 3:1 against #111111 (icons). “Distance” is a color-difference score (CIEDE2000): under about 5 reads as the same color at a glance, and 10 or more is clearly different. Each row shows all five red marks in that red, at 48px and 16px.

#B8283A

Crimson Recommended

White on it: 6.17:1 · on #111111: 3.06:1 · distance from Redfin 8.8, Airbnb 16.3 (nearest: Cornell red 7.6, Redfin 8.8)

#CC0034

Signal

White on it: 5.81:1 · on #111111: 3.25:1 · distance from Redfin 6.9, Airbnb 14.4 (nearest: Redfin 6.9, Airbnb #E61E4D 7.5)

#B84838

Brick

White on it: 5.22:1 · on #111111: 3.62:1 · distance from Redfin 8.3, Airbnb 16.0 (nearest: Cornell red 7.7, HotPads orange 7.8)

Red options

Files use Crimson #B8283A. 01, 03 and 05 are the round 1 ideas redrawn for red; 06 and 07 are new, based on the research below.

01

Tick V

The V is the check

Same idea as round 1: the V in the name is a verified check. As an app icon it’s now a white check-V on a red tile, which looks the most like a finished app icon of the five.

01-tick-red-mark.svg · 01-tick-red-lockup.svg

03

Mates M Recommended

An M made of two mates and a check

Two upright strokes (two roommates) joined by a red check form an M. The tile is now a white card (the app’s borderless white card) with a black M, so the only red is the check: one small red accent, the same way the app uses its one green. One risk to check: a red M on a white tile is also what Gmail’s old icon looked like, though ours is asymmetric and mostly black.

03-mcheck-red-mark.svg · 03-mcheck-red-lockup.svg

05

Verified name

The name wears the badge

The dot on the i becomes a red verified badge, and the app icon is a V with that badge. It’s still the quietest option, but in red it clashes with the app: in-app verified badges are green, so the logo would show “verified” in a different color.

05-badge-red-mark.svg · 05-badge-red-lockup.svg

06

Seal

A wax seal

New. Red wax seals have long been the mark that proves a document is real, which gives a reason for the red itself. The scalloped seal carries the check-V. Nobody in rental or roommate apps uses a seal; social apps use similar scalloped badges, though always in blue. Its weak spot is the app itself: next to green verified badges it looks like a second, red verified badge.

06-seal-red-mark.svg · 06-seal-red-lockup.svg

07

Pair

Two pieces, one check

New. Two rounded pieces, a short black one and a long red one, lean together into a single check: two people who fit, verified. It’s the lightest mark and uses no house, pin, key or heart. It’s also the most abstract, so it relies more on the name next to it.

  • Blue owns the categoryBlue, teal or navy is the main color for 14 of the 20 (Zillow, Trulia, Zumper, Rent., SpareRoom, Roomie, Badi, Furnished Finder and every student-housing operator). Red sets Verimate apart immediately.
  • Red has two ownersRedfin (bright red #DE3341, wordmark in a red tile) and Airbnb (pink-red #FF385C, rounded loop). Avoid a bright pure red, a pink-red, and a red tile with only the wordmark in it.
  • The house is a clichéSix use a roof or house shape (Zillow, Trulia, Zumper, SpareRoom, Roomie, Roomster). Hearts stand for “match” (Zumper, Roomster), and there’s also a key (Furnished Finder), a pin-like loop (Airbnb), a frog mascot and a bed illustration.
  • Nobody shows trustNo one uses a check, a seal or an ID: verification is our gap. Student-housing operators look like property companies (navy serif monograms, a compass), not like something students would pick.
  • Wordmark-only can workRedfin, Rent. and Badi lead with the name alone. Rent.’s colored period is close to our 05 idea (a colored dot on the wordmark), which is another reason 05 is weaker.
  • Local reds to keep clear ofCornell’s carnelian (#B31B1B) is an hour away in Ithaca, and SU orange (#F76900) is SU’s own. None of the three reds is within 7 of Cornell, and all stay well away from orange.
