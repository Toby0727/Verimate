## What shipped today

**Sign-in and the survey**
- Real Google sign-in through Supabase. School accounts (@g.syr.edu, @esf.edu) are verified and get +5% raffle odds. Personal Google accounts can join too and are marked unverified.
- The six steps:
  1. Name, gender, optional photo (drag and zoom to crop), year (including 5th year, Grad 1st–3rd, PhD), major, and an optional referral code.
  2. Monthly budget; more than one can be picked.
  3. Where to live: a tappable map on a white, label-free basemap (MapLibre + OpenFreeMap).
  4. Study abroad: pick every term away, with a destination for each term from SU's real programs (LA, NYC, DC, the five SU Abroad centers, partner cities, semester at sea).
  5. Sublet and roommate timing, as a term-by-need grid. Terms away from step 4 are tagged with the place and "Sublet my place" starts ticked.
  6. Lifestyle (drinking, smoking or vaping, weed, Greek life, pets), each with an icon.
- **Done screen:** the student's profile card, entry count, a confetti burst, and their referral card with a code and share link. Every friend who finishes on a school account adds +10%.

**Raffle and growth**
- $50 Amazon gift card. Entries close November 30, 2026. Entries are 1 for finishing plus 1 for a photo; school account +5%; +10% per school-account friend. Official rules are at `/raffle-rules`.
- A first month of Verimate free for everyone on the waitlist.
- The homepage leads with the slogan "Building the most complete sublet + roommate matching for off-campus living", the sign-in button, a crimson gift card with a live seconds countdown, and three perks.
